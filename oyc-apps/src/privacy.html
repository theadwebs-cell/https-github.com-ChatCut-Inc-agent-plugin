Privacy Policy

On Your Call (OYC) · Hyderabad · Last updated 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information On Your Call ("OYC", "we", "us") collects through the OYC customer app, the OYC Partner app, our website onyourcall.in and WhatsApp, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to customers who book home services and to service professionals ("partners") who work with OYC.

1. Information we collect

Customers

  • Contact details: your name and mobile number.
  • Service address: the address and landmark you enter. If you tap "Use my current location", your device's location is used once to fill in the address and show it on a map. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Push notifications: if you allow them in the app, we store a notification token for your device to send booking updates. You can turn them off in your phone settings.
  • Booking details: the services you choose, date and time slot, notes for the professional, amount, payment method, booking status, and any rating or review you give.

Service partners

  • Identity and verification: name, mobile number, 12-digit Aadhaar number, a passport-size photo, and photos of the front and back of your Aadhaar card.
  • Work details: services you offer, work area, notes about your experience, your login PIN, your online/offline status, and the jobs you accept and complete (including the amount collected).
  • Live location (while on the way): when you tap "Start trip – I'm on the way", the Partner app shares your device's location, including in the background, so the customer can follow your arrival on a map. Sharing stops automatically when you start the job, and we keep only your latest position, for a few hours.

Collected automatically

  • Basic technical information needed for the app to work, such as the information your browser or device sends when it connects to our service. Your login, cart and saved addresses are stored on your own device so you stay signed in.

2. How we use your information

  • To take, confirm, schedule and complete your bookings, including contacting you by phone or WhatsApp.
  • To assign a suitable professional and share with them what they need to do the job.
  • To verify the identity of service partners before they receive jobs, for the safety of our customers.
  • To show booking status, start codes and job history in the apps.
  • To keep records of payments collected and to settle partner earnings.
  • To answer support requests, prevent misuse and improve our services.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Who we share it with

  • Between customer and partner: when a professional is assigned, they see your name, mobile number, service address, landmark, notes and booking details. You see the professional's name and mobile number. Your 4-digit start code is never shown to the partner — you share it when they arrive. While the professional is on the way, you can see their live location on a map.
  • Service providers we use to run OYC: Google (Google Sheets and Google Drive store booking records and partner documents), WhatsApp (messages you choose to send us), Google Maps and OpenStreetMap (showing and looking up addresses), and our website host FlexiFunnels.
  • Legal reasons: if required by law, or to protect the safety of our customers, partners or the public.

Partner Aadhaar numbers and document photos are used only by OYC for verification. They are never shown to customers.

4. Storage and security

Booking and partner records are stored in a private Google Sheet, and partner documents in a private Google Drive folder, both owned by OYC and accessible only to authorised OYC staff. The admin panel is protected by a password. Data travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

5. How long we keep it

  • Booking records: for as long as needed to provide the service, handle complaints, and meet accounting and legal requirements.
  • Partner verification documents: while you are an active partner, and deleted within 90 days after you stop working with OYC, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.
  • Information on your device (login, cart, addresses) stays until you log out, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can view your bookings at any time in the app, and cancel a booking before a professional is on the way.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it (see below).
  • You can turn off location access in your phone settings and type your address instead.

7. Delete your account and data

To delete your OYC account and personal data (customer or partner):

  1. Send a WhatsApp message to +91 98666 89196 saying "Please delete my OYC account", from your registered mobile number (or include it in the message).
  2. We confirm the request with you and delete your profile, saved bookings data and, for partners, your Aadhaar number and document photos, within 30 days.

We may keep a minimal record of completed paid jobs (date, service and amount, without your documents) where the law requires it for tax or accounting. You can also log out of the app at any time, which removes your login from that device.

8. Children

OYC is meant for adults. Bookings must be made by someone aged 18 or over, and partners must be at least 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. Important changes will be shown in the app.
